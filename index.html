Security audit

Who can reach what — across the CRM and the tenant behind it. The answer is usually "more people than you think."

  • Security roles, business units, field-level profiles
  • Permissions that accumulated over the years and never left
  • Conditional Access gaps and legacy authentication
  • Admin accounts that outlived the person who needed them
  • Apps someone approved once, in a hurry

Licence optimization

Licences measured against actual use. Most estates I look at have 15–25% recoverable, and nobody is happy to hear it.

  • Inactive accounts still costing money every month
  • People on a tier three steps above what they open
  • The same person licensed twice, in two subsidiaries
  • Savings quantified per year, in your currency

CRM health check

What the environment actually looks like underneath — before it becomes a migration problem and everyone's problem.

  • Configuration drift since the original build
  • Duplicate records and data integrity
  • Dataverse audit settings and retention
  • Power Platform governance and DLP policies
